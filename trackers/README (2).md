## 📋 Trackers

Both trackers are organized around the **just-in-time (JIT)** philosophy — rather than dumping everything into a single flat checklist, items are sequenced by the assembly step that first needs them. This way you know exactly what to buy or print before you sit down to build each section, and nothing gets forgotten in a pile.

---

### 🛒 `Voron_2.4_JIT_Purchase_Tracker.xlsx`

A purchasing tracker built from the BOM generated by the [Voron Configurator](https://vorondesign.com/voron2.4). All 122 BOM line items are sorted by the assembly step at which they're first required, so you can place focused orders as the build progresses rather than buying everything at once.

**Key features:**

- **When to Buy** column maps each part to its first-needed assembly step (Frame → Z Drive → AB Drive → Gantry → Toolhead → ... → Bed → Wiring)
- **⚡ Buy All Now** indicator (gold rows) flags items that are used across multiple assembly stages — fasteners, bearings, heat-set inserts, connector pins, motors, grease, etc. The logic: if a part shows up in step 2 but you'll also need it in steps 4, 7, and 12, buy the full BOM quantity at step 2 rather than placing repeat orders
- Per-line unit price entry with auto-calculated totals and a grand total
- Status dropdown: `Research → Ordered → Received`
- Order number, vendor, and date tracking columns
- **Buy List by Step** sheet — a condensed checklist version of the same data, organized by assembly stage, suitable for printing
- **Key & Tips** sheet — explains the buy-all logic and summarizes what happens at each of the 14 assembly steps

> ⚠️ **Note:** This tracker was created with the assistance of [Claude](https://claude.ai) (Anthropic) and has not yet been fully verified against the official Voron documentation. Cross-check quantities and part specifications against the [Voron Sourcing Guide](https://vorondesign.com/sourcing_guide?model=V2.4) and your Configurator-generated BOM before ordering.

### 🖨️ `Voron_2.4_Print_Tracker.xlsx`

A printed parts tracker organized by assembly manual order, allowing for just-in-time printing of parts as the build progresses. Inspired by and built from the excellent Gist by [**cmidgley**](https://gist.github.com/cmidgley/10423bbae72dacb02c46926f60acb7d2) — full credit to them for organizing the part list by assembly order and documenting the select-one options clearly. That Gist was the direct source for part names, assembly step sequencing, and the JIT printing concept.

**Key features:**

- 120 parts across 14 assembly steps, sequenced to match the [Voron 2.4 Assembly Manual](https://github.com/VoronDesign/Voron-2/blob/Voron2.4/Docs/2.4_Assembly_Manual.pdf)
- **Color column** — highlights whether each part prints in your primary or accent color (`[a]_` prefix parts)
- **Select Group column** — clearly flags parts where you must choose only one option (e.g., E3D vs. Dragon vs. Mosquito printhead, 6mm vs. 9mm Z idler, generic vs. IGUS chain anchor). Purple row highlight makes these easy to spot
- **STL Link** — clickable link to the GitHub directory for each part's STL file
- Status dropdown: `Queue → Printing → Done → Reprint → Not Needed`
- Date printed and print time columns for tracking progress
- **Summary** sheet — step-by-step part counts with a running done/remaining tally
- **Key & Tips** sheet — official Voron print settings (40% infill, 4 walls, 5 top/bottom layers, 0.2mm layer height, ABS/ASA required, no supports), plus notes on r2 vs. original Gist differences

> ⚠️ **Note:** The source Gist is based on the original Afterburner toolhead. Voron 2.4r2 uses the **Stealthburner + Clockwork 2** toolhead, whose STLs live in the separate [Voron-Stealthburner repository](https://github.com/VoronDesign/Voron-Stealthburner). The Stealthburner step in the tracker notes this. The rest of the assembly steps are accurate for r2.
>
> This tracker was created with the assistance of [Claude](https://claude.ai) (Anthropic) and has not yet been fully verified against the official Voron documentation.
